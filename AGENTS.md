# Free My TV: guide for AI assistants

The full guide is [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): read it first and follow it (it is written for any assistant, not only
Claude Code), and the step-by-step guide it points to in `guide/` for the task (installing on Google TV, LG or Samsung,
another TV, a release). Most people ask in plain words, like "Install Free My TV on my TV at 192.168.1.50. I live in
Lisbon.", and don't know what a terminal is: explain each step simply, and ask before anything that changes their TV or
computer.
